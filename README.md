# Signable.ca

Canada-wide commercial and industrial signage supply and contracting.

**Scope:** All indoor and outdoor signage used in commercial, industrial, institutional, retail, hospitality, healthcare, construction and public facilities. Supply, custom fabrication coordination, installation, repair, replacement, maintenance and multi-location programs.

**Operating model:** Direct customer contracting and project management, using qualified fabrication and installation partners where appropriate. Not a directory, marketplace or referral network. Do not imply owned factories, local offices or in-house installation teams without evidence.

**Market:** Canada-wide, bilingual English / Canadian French, quote-first.

**Status:** Planning and Phase 1A instructions uploaded. No website or logo assets have been created yet. Trademark clearance and operational compliance remain open.
