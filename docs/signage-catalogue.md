# Complete Commercial Facility Signage Catalogue

Organize discovery by **sign type**, **facility/industry**, and **service**.

## Products
- Exterior and building: channel letters, illuminated signs, fascia, monument, pylon, cabinet, address, building ID.
- Interior and architectural: reception, office, room, door, directories, nameplates, plaques, wall graphics.
- Safety and regulatory: hazard, PPE, WHMIS, chemical, machinery, lockout, restricted area, warning and mandatory instruction.
- Fire and emergency: exit, evacuation, assembly point, extinguisher, emergency equipment, first aid.
- Warehouse and logistics: rack labels, aisle markers, dock numbers, forklift/pedestrian routes, loading bays.
- Parking and traffic: accessible, reserved, loading, speed, traffic, directional and property control.
- Accessibility: tactile, Braille, accessible washroom, wayfinding and compliant identification.
- Property management: tenant, directory, surveillance, no trespassing, site rules and visitor signage.
- Construction: hoarding, temporary safety, project, fence graphics and traffic control.
- Retail/hospitality: menus, promotional, point of sale, window graphics and digital displays.
- Healthcare/education/institutions: departments, rooms, campus wayfinding, hygiene and visitor signs.
- Utilities/environment: electrical hazards, confined spaces, pipeline markers, environmental notices.
- Specialty: engraved, reflective, metal, acrylic, magnetic, vinyl, vehicle/fleet, banners, electronic message centres.

## Services
Supply-only; custom sign design; fabrication coordination; permits and engineering coordination; delivery; installation; electrical sign work through appropriately qualified parties; repairs; cleaning; maintenance; removal; replacement; national multi-location programs.

## Industries
Commercial offices, facilities management, industrial and manufacturing, warehousing, retail chains, restaurants, hotels, property management, construction, hospitals, schools, government and public facilities.

## Compliance
Research applicable Canadian federal/provincial/territorial rules, WHMIS, accessibility, fire/building codes, municipal sign bylaws and permits by location and sign type. US OSHA requirements must not be presented as governing Canadian facilities. Avoid unsupported claims that any sign is compliant or certified.
