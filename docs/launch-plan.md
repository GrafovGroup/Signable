# Signable.ca Launch Plan

## Commercial model
Direct B2B signage supplier and contractor across Canada. Quote-first, no public pricing by default. Manage customer requirements, estimating, supplier procurement, installation, quality and aftercare. Qualified partners can execute work; no misleading in-house claims.

## Phases
- Phase 0: Canadian market/competitor research, trademark and common-law screening (including Signable.com), demand validation, supplier coverage and regulatory review.
- Phase 1A: Five distinct original logo concepts, visual identity, bilingual positioning, design system and documentation. **Owner chooses logo before Phase 1B.**
- Phase 1B: Full site architecture, conversion copy, product/service/industry taxonomy.
- Phase 1C: English and Québec-appropriate French content, navigation, forms, metadata and language switcher.
- Phase 1D: Responsive accessible technical implementation.
- Phase 1E: QA including mobile, forms, performance, accessibility, SEO and bilingual links.
- Phase 1F: Production hosting and launch, subject to approvals.
- Phase 2: Commercial activation: verify info@signable.ca, reuse only verified Grafov Group 833 phone, form delivery and synthetic tests, quote handling.
- Phase 3: Distinct useful pages for Canadian provinces, territories, major cities and surrounding metros; no doorway pages or fake offices.
- Phase 4: Supplier and installer coverage, maintenance contracts, conversion optimization and national account sales.

## Intake
Request a Quote and Call Now. Collect name, company, email, phone, location, sign type, dimensions, quantities, supply-only vs installed, scope, date, drawings/photos and multi-location requirement. Do not claim forms work until tested.

## Launch gates
Trademark clearance; fabrication and installation capabilities; insurance; electrical/structural permits and licensed trades as applicable; verified contact details; customer data/privacy controls; tested quote delivery.
