# SIGNABLE.CA — CODEX MASTER PROMPT
## Phase 0 + Phase 1A: Research, Brand Identity and Design Foundation

**Repository:** GrafovGroup/Signable
**Business:** Canadian commercial and industrial signage supply and contracting
**Coverage:** Canada-wide from launch, English and Canadian French
**Owner:** Grafov Group

## Role
Act as a senior Canadian B2B brand strategist, signage industry researcher, creative director, UX designer, SEO strategist and technical lead. Work autonomously through research and Phase 1A; stop for owner logo approval before Phase 1B.

## Positioning
Signable.ca is a **direct provider** of virtually every type of indoor and outdoor sign used in commercial facilities. It supplies standard and custom signs, coordinates fabrication, installation, permits, repairs, maintenance, replacement and multi-site rollouts. It may use qualified execution partners. Never call it a broker, referral network, directory or marketplace. Never invent staff, factories, offices, projects, credentials or nationwide physical presence.

**Suggested tagline:** Commercial Signs. Nationwide.

## Phase 0 — Research
1. Analyze Canadian commercial/industrial signage demand, competitors, customer segments and search intent.
2. Research the existing Signable.com e-signature brand, Canadian trademark records and common-law conflicts; document risks, do not claim legal clearance.
3. Map commercial suppliers, fabricators, installers, sign permitting and licensed electrical/structural requirements.
4. Identify appropriate Canadian and Québec French terminology and relevant accessibility, WHMIS, fire and safety frameworks.
5. Write docs/phase-0-research.md with sources, dates, risks and recommendations.

## Full catalogue — mandatory
Read docs/signage-catalogue.md and include ALL categories: exterior/building, illuminated, interior/office, architectural, safety, WHMIS, fire/emergency, warehouse/industrial, parking/traffic, wayfinding, accessibility/Braille, property, construction, retail/hospitality, healthcare/institutional, utilities/environmental, fleet, digital and specialty. Cover temporary and permanent, standardized and custom signs. Do not narrow the brand to storefront signage or printing.

## Five original logo concepts — mandatory
1. Architectural wordmark: geometric, signage-inspired typography.
2. Industrial monogram: dimensional S, structural and fabrication cues.
3. Illuminated identity: restrained channel-letter/lighting inspiration.
4. Sign-panel emblem: commercial sign structure/frame concept.
5. Premium minimalist: enduring corporate wordmark.

**Important:** All five must suit the entire commercial facility signage market, not just illuminated storefront signs. Make five genuinely different visual directions, not recolours.

For each: horizontal, stacked, icon-only, full-colour, black and white, light/dark previews, editable SVG master, PNG exports, favicon concept, palette, typography and commercial rationale. Produce a visual comparison page with actual renderable artwork.

## Design system
Explore differentiated palettes (navy/silver, charcoal/electric blue, graphite/orange, black/steel, dark blue/teal). Define type scale, accessible colour contrast, spacing, buttons, navigation, quote forms, iconography, photography, responsive rules and components. Avoid clones of other Grafov Group brands, generic template look and invented testimonials.

## Site architecture foundation
Design three parallel navigation paths:
1. **Shop by Sign Type** (complete catalogue)
2. **Shop by Facility / Industry** (offices, warehouses, factories, construction, retail, hospitality, healthcare, government, etc.)
3. **Shop by Service** (supply, fabrication, design, installation, repair, maintenance, removal and national rollouts)

Include Products, Services, Industries, Locations, About and Request a Quote. Plan useful distinct local pages for major Canadian cities, provinces, territories and surrounding metros without keyword stuffing or fake locations.

## Commercial UX
Primary CTA **Request a Quote**, secondary **Call Now**. No published prices unless approved. Proposed email info@signable.ca must be verified. Reuse existing Grafov Group 833 phone only if exact number is verified; never invent it. Plan quote fields for company, name, email, phone, location, category, quantity, dimensions, installation requirement, scope, timing, attachments and multiple locations. No claims of functional delivery until tested.
